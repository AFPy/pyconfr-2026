Visa

Check if You Need a Visa

If you plan to attend PyConFR 2026 in Biarritz, please check if you need a visa to enter France and apply as early as possible.

France is part of the Schengen area. If you already have a valid Schengen visa, you do not need to apply for an additional one. If in doubt, please contact your consulate or embassy.

You can also visit the France Visas website to check if you need a visa.

Invitation Letter

If you need a visa, we can provide an invitation letter for your application.

Invitation letter requests should be sent via email to secretaire@afpy.org. Please include the following information in your request:

  • Title (Mr./Ms.),
  • Full name,
  • Date of birth,
  • Nationality,
  • Passport number,
  • Title of your presentation at the event (if applicable),
  • Amount of the grant provided by AFPy (if applicable),
  • Name of the sponsoring company you represent (if applicable).

Getting to the Campus

By Public Transport

The school is located on Izarbel technopole which is served by bus lines 21 and 8 from Txik Txak bus network (timetables are given for information purpose only but won't be the ones in place on the dates of the event).

By Train

The closest train station is Biarritz one, linked to Bordeaux TGV (high-speed train) station and then to the main French lines.

From Biarritz train station, you can take bus line 8 or 21 to Izarbel stop to get to the school in about 20 minutes.

By Coach

Biarritz is directly reachable from dozens of towns in France and Europe by FlixBus Coach.

By Car

Izarbel technopole is close to A63 highway, next to exit nulmber 4.

You're coming by car and you have room? Consider carsharing :).

By Plane

From Biarritz airport, you can take bus line 8 or 21 to Izarbel stop to get to the school in about 15 minutes.

Food Advice

For Saturday & Sunday lunches, there will be two food trucks on the campus:

  • Voyage culinaire for regional food.
  • El Güero for mexican food.

For Thursday & Friday lunches or dinners, you can find LE SPOT' (500m from the campus), this is the same restaurant that hosts the Saturday night dinner.

Accommodation Advice

The ideal location for accomodation is on Izarbel technopole or around Biarritz train station (3km from the campus and from Biarritz downtown).

The school hosting the event (ESTIA) offers conditioned partnerships with various hotels in the area:

  • Fasthôtel Biarritz Bidart Côte Basque (1km from the campus), rooms from €80 per night: 15% discount with ESTIA promotion code.
  • Kaliko Bidart hotel (5km from the campus), rooms from €85 per night with ESTIA 2026 promotion code.
  • Jules Verne Biarritz hotel (5km from the campus), rooms from €80 per night.
  • Kameris Biarritz hotel (5km from the campus), rooms from €120 per night.
